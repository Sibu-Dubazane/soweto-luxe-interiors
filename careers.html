Work With Us

We’re building a trusted network of skilled trades across Gauteng — professionals who deliver quality on residential and commercial interior and finishing projects.

Project-based partnerships

These are contract, per-job engagements — not full-time employment. When a client project needs your trade, we assign you to that job, agree scope and rate upfront, and you are paid on completion of the project.

  • Matched to live jobs — work that fits your trade and location
  • Paid per project — clear expectations before you start onsite
  • Room to grow with us — reliable, quality-focused trades get repeat assignments as new projects come in

How to register

  1. State the trade or role you are registering for.
  2. Include your full name and contact number.
  3. Share your years of experience and areas you can work (e.g. Soweto, greater Gauteng).
  4. Indicate when you’re available for onsite project work (days or weeks you can take assignments).
  5. Attach or mention your CV or portfolio — photos of completed work are welcome for trade roles.
